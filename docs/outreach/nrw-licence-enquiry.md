Hello,

I am building a small free public web app that lists the nearest publicly accessible forest sites in
Great Britain and hands the chosen one to a phone's map app. It has no adverts, no tracking and no
sign-up, and it works with no mobile signal, which is the point of it. It is here:
https://forestlocator.enhanceify.co.uk/

I would like to include your recreation data, specifically **NRW_GB_RECREATION_POINTS** and
**NRW_GB_RECREATION_AREAS** as published on DataMapWales, and I have found two statements about them
that I cannot reconcile.

Your own metadata record, **NRW_DS116288**, states that these are available under the Open
Government Licence with attribution, and that there are no access restrictions on the data.

The data.gov.uk entry for the same dataset states that it is used internally for recreation
management and should not be used in internet applications or publications without Natural Resources
Wales' prior approval.

My app is a public internet application, so those two sentences give opposite answers. Could you
confirm which applies? **If prior approval is what is needed, please treat this email as that
request.**

Attribution would read: "Contains Natural Resources Wales information © Natural Resources Wales and
Database Right."

I am happy to word the credit differently if you would prefer particular wording, and happy to
answer any questions about how the data would be used.

Thank you,

Rob Craig
r@enhanceify.co.uk

## Why this email exists, for whoever picks it up next

**The contradiction is the whole point.** Do not soften it into a general "may I use your data"
question. Naming both statements and asking which governs is what makes a definite answer cheap for
them to give, and a definite answer is what card `0017` is waiting on.

**The last sentence of the third paragraph is doing real work.** Offering to treat the email as the
approval request itself means a "you need approval" reply can also be the approval, rather than
starting a second round trip.

**What each outcome unlocks**, both set out on card `0017`:

- A written yes naming the licence unlocks option 2, the full recreation dataset.
- A no, or silence by the recheck date, means option 1, which needs nobody's permission.
