**To:** info@forestryengland.uk
**Subject:** Your forest finder, from a phone in a car park

Hello,

Finding the nearest forest on your website from a phone means typing a forest, place or postcode
first. That is hard to do in a car with not much signal, so I built a small free app that does
that one job.

It is here: https://forestlocator.enhanceify.co.uk/

The picture attached shows your finder and the app side by side, on the same phone, in the same
place.

The app opens with the nearest forests first, works with no signal at all, and sends people to the
site they choose in Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze with one tap. Every forest links back to its own
page on your website.

The car park locations come from the open data you publish, and the forest details from your
website, under the Open Government Licence. Forestry England is credited in the app. I have not used
your logos or photographs, and there is no sign-up, tracking or advertising.

I am not asking for anything. If you would like anything changed, or have any concern about it, I
would be glad to hear.

Many Thanks,

Robert Craig
https://forestlocator.enhanceify.co.uk/
