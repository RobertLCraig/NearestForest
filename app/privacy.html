Privacy policy: Forest Locator

Last updated 4 November 2026.

Forest Locator is a free app that lists forests, car parks and campsites in Great Britain, nearest first. It is made by Enhanceify Ltd (company number 16107718), E7 Marine Gate, Marine Drive, Brighton BN2 5TP. Questions: r@enhanceify.co.uk.

Your location

The app asks for your location to sort the list by distance. That happens on your phone. Your location is kept on your phone so distances show before a new fix arrives. The app does not send your location to us or to anyone else.

What we do not collect

There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not ask for your name, email address or contacts.

What our web server sees

The app is a web page from forestlocator.enhanceify.co.uk. Like every web server, ours (hosted by Hostinger) receives your internet (IP) address and the files your phone asks for. The hosting company keeps standard access logs of these requests. We do not use them to identify you.

Map tiles (off unless you turn them on). While the Tiles layer is on, map images come through our server from Thunderforest. Our server then sees which part of the map you are looking at. Thunderforest sees our server, not you. To stop overuse, our server counts tiles per address per day. It stores a salted one-way hash of your address, not the address itself. Counts from earlier days are deleted as the app is used.

iPhone Shortcut. If you use the optional Siri Shortcut, it sends your position to our server to find the nearest sites. Our server does not store it, but the request appears in the hosting access log.

Other apps and sites

When you tap Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze, that app gets the site you picked. When you open a site's own web page, that site gets the visit. Their own privacy policies apply. Your position is not passed to them.

Children

The app is not aimed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what we hold about you and ask us to delete it. Email r@enhanceify.co.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
