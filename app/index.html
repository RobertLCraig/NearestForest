    Your location stays on this phone. It sorts the list and is remembered here so distances still show before a fresh fix arrives. There are no accounts, no tracking and no analytics, and nothing you do is sent anywhere. The one exception is the optional Tiles layer on the map: while it is on, map images are fetched through this site, which means the server sees which part of the map you are looking at. It is off unless you turn it on.
